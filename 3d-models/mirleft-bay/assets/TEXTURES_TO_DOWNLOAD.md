# Textures to download (free, CC0, Poly Haven)

On each page: choose **2K**, format **JPG**, and download the maps **Diffuse (Color), Rough, Nor GL (Normal), Disp (Displacement)**. Download only the folder named in the "Folder" column; each folder holds the 4 maps.
Put the folders in the 3D-MODELS repo under `textures/` (or send them in the chat) and tell me.

| # | Use in the model | Poly Haven texture (page) | Folder |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Walls (sand render). I tint it to the site colour. | **white_plaster_rough_01**: https://polyhaven.com/a/white_plaster_rough_01 | `textures/white_plaster_rough_01/` |
| 2 | Garden walls, retaining walls, canal sides | **stacked_stone_wall**: https://polyhaven.com/a/stacked_stone_wall | `textures/stacked_stone_wall/` |
| 3 | Pergolas, timber lattice | **wood_planks**: https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_planks | `textures/wood_planks/` |
| 4 | Roads and sidewalks (pavers) | **patterned_paving**: https://polyhaven.com/a/patterned_paving | `textures/patterned_paving/` |
| 5 | Terraces, pool decks, roof terraces | **marble_tiles** (beige square tiles): https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_tiles | `textures/marble_tiles/` |
| 6 | Ground around the site (aerial shots) | **aerial_ground_rock**: https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_ground_rock | `textures/aerial_ground_rock/` |
| 7 | Beach | **aerial_beach_01**: https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_beach_01 | `textures/aerial_beach_01/` |

Alternatives if you prefer another look:

- **Stone:** `old_stone_wall` (beige-brown).
- **Wood:** `wooden_planks` (dry, matte).
- **Pavers:** `stone_pavers`.
