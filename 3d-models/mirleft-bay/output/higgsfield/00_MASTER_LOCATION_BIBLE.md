# LOCATION BIBLE — Mirleft Bay (continuity document, not a redesign)

**Place.** A gated resort on a spur of ochre plateau above the Atlantic, near Mirleft (Sidi Ifni), Morocco. The spur runs WNW (ocean) to ESE (road R104), about 650 m long, between two dry ravines. To the north-west there is a long sandy bay with surf. To the south, a dark rocky coast. Inland, a bare plateau with low argan scrub and distant hills.

**Architecture.** Contemporary southern-Moroccan language:
- Simple cubic volumes in hand-trowelled sand render.
- Slightly darker ochre surrounds around every opening.
- A row of small square niches at the top of the ground floor.
- Timber pergolas over the terraces, and geometric timber lattice screens.
- Flat roofs with parapets and roof terraces with round jacuzzis.
- Public buildings are kasbah-like: crenellated parapets, corner towers, recessed vertical window bays.

**Units.** About 88 buildings in total:
- 34 R+1 duplex in mirrored pairs (the hero unit).
- 33 detached R+1 villas with a pool.
- 13 single-storey villas with a pool.
- 4 large single-storey villas.
- A hotel and a spa.
- The Pavillon (club house) with two free-form pools.
- A reception building with towers.
- A park with three oval lake basins and a palm island at the ocean end.

**Ground.** Beige concrete pavers on every road and sidewalk, honed limestone terraces and pool decks, green lawns, clipped hedges, stone retaining walls at the plot edges.

**Vegetation.** Washingtonia fan palms along the streets and in the gardens, date palms on the central avenue, at the club pools and in the lake park, silver olive trees in the gardens, tufts of pale ornamental grass by the pools. Outside the walls, only sparse grey-green argan scrub.

**Materials.** Sand render #D2B289, ochre surrounds #B79473, timber #C7A47E, bronze aluminium frames #433C37, clear glass with warm lit interiors and linen curtains, pool water pale turquoise.

**Climate / season.** Dry, clear, mild. Mid-October.

**Light (locked).** Golden hour. The sun is low in the WSW (azimuth 257°, elevation about 10°), warm and raking, with long shadows to the ENE. A soft warm haze sits on the horizon, and interior lamps are just switched on.
