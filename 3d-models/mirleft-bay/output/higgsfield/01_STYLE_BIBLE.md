# STYLE BIBLE

- **Identity:** calm, editorial luxury real-estate film. "Un moment de vie. Rien d'autre."
- **Photography:** natural, soft contrast, warm highlights, open shadows, no crushed blacks. Architecture photographed straight: vertical lines stay vertical.
- **Colour temperature:** warm (about 3800-4500 K look) on sunlit surfaces, cool-neutral in the shadows, peach-to-pale-blue sky.
- **Material mood:** matte render, warm timber, soft stone, glossy water. No plastic sheen.
- **Atmosphere:** light warm haze on the distance only. No fog in the foreground.
- **Camera language:** slow, deliberate moves: dolly, push-in, lateral track, gentle crane, aerial drift. 24-35 mm for places, 35-50 mm for human scale, 50-70 mm for details.
- **Depth of field:** deep for architecture, shallow only on detail shots.
- **Lighting philosophy:** one sun, physically placed for the site. Practical lamps glow inside. No invented spotlights.
- **Realism level:** photoreal. AI motion must not "improve" the design.
- **AI treatment:** Higgsfield adds motion only (palms, water, people, light shimmer, camera move) on top of a locked Blender hero frame.
- **Format:** vertical 9:16, 1080 × 1920 (master hero frames at the same size).
